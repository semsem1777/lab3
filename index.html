The best movie I've watched lately

The best movie I've watched lately has to be the F1 movie, ever since I watched that movie for the 3rd time, I keep trying to get my friends to all
watch it. There's something that's so different about the movie. It's not the star-studded cast, it's not the amazing plot, or the amazing soundtrack.
It may be the score. I say that because every moment, every emotion was felt the way it needed to be and I think that's down to how great the score was.
It had me watching live orchestra renditions of it. It's Hans Zimmer anyway so it didn't come as much of a surprise but still. It was such a great
collection of sounds. I feel that tied it all together, it was like the final piece of the movie that made it worthy to be a 10. I felt that way from start
to finish.
